What counts as rent-burdened?

Households spending 30% or more of their income on gross rent. The tract share is burdened households divided by renter households for whom the ACS computed a rent-to-income ratio; "not computed" households are excluded from that denominator.

Read the map with care

These are estimates from the 2020-2024 ACS 5-year survey, not a count of every household. A tract's estimate has a margin of error, and small differences between neighboring tracts may not be meaningful. Map boundaries: U.S. Census TIGER/Line.
